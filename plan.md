# Road to CSEC 2027 Maths: preparation plan for Arjun Ramdeen

Goal: At least Grade II in CSEC Mathematics, May-June 2027, aiming for Grade I. Three one-hour sessions a week, 24 Nov 2026 to 30 Apr 2027; one episode video per session. Study material is the published course (https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUVKbsZosp2Q) and the May-June 2026 Paper 2 worked solutions (https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLMlZ7s_Gxf0Y). Tutor: Miss Morrison, who also tutors Ashanti Reid for English A (https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLXU28KFyF9wc).

## Weaknesses and the steps to fix them

**Algebra.** Evidence: Wrote 3(x - 4) = 3x - 4 and -3 - 5 = 2; could not start x² + 5x + 6.

- Directed numbers first: say each sign aloud and use the number line
- Multiply the bracket's every term: draw the arrows
- Ten minutes of algebra every day, like nets: short, sharp, daily
- Course lessons: 16 (Algebra Basics and Directed Numbers), 17 (Expanding, Factorising and Binary Operations), 25 (Factorising Quadratics and Changing the Subject)

**Word problems.** Evidence: Left the bat-and-ball problem blank: 'I don't know where to start'.

- Underline every number and what it measures
- Name the unknown: 'let x be ...'; write one equation
- Check the answer against the story, not just the equation
- Course lessons: 19 (Linear Equations and Inequalities), 26 (Simultaneous Linear Equations)

**Functions and graphs.** Evidence: Plotted (3, 2) at (2, 3); could not find the gradient of a line.

- x first: walk along the corridor, then up the stairs
- Gradient = rise over run: count squares, check the sign
- Course lessons: 21 (Linear Graphs), 30 (Gradient and the Equation of a Line)

**Geometry and trigonometry.** Evidence: Used sine for an adjacent-hypotenuse pair; gave angle facts with no reasons.

- Label O, A and H from the angle before choosing SOH CAH TOA
- Every angle step gets a reason in brackets
- Course lessons: 34 (Symmetry, Angles and Polygons), 36 (Pythagoras and the Trigonometric Ratios)

**Measurement.** Evidence: Mixed cm and m in one area; gave a volume in square units.

- Convert every length to one unit before you calculate
- Units check: length cm, area cm², volume cm³
- Course lessons: 12 (Units, Time and Speed), 13 (Perimeter and Area), 14 (Surface Area and Volume)

## Habits

**Distraction.** Evidence: Phone on the desk; 3 WhatsApp checks in the first ten minutes; studies only the night before tests.

- Phone in another room for each 45-minute block
- Study like nets: same time, every day, short and focused

**Confidence.** Evidence: 'I'm not a maths person'; gives up on a question after one try.

- Keep a wins list: one question solved every day
- Read a blank as 'not yet', and try one first step
