Episode 3: Algebra Nets: Signs and Brackets

Watch on YouTube

Algebra nets. Arjun's sign errors and bracket errors come from half-remembered rules. We fix directed numbers with his team's net run rate, then expand brackets with an arrow to every term.

In this session

  • Directed numbers on a number line, using net run rate
  • Where 'two minuses make a plus' really applies
  • An exam-style question: an overdrawn shop account
  • Expanding brackets, including a negative outside the bracket
  • Your turn: three to try, with the model answer
  • A first look at factorising, and his maths nets routine
