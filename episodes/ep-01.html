Episode 1: Meet Arjun

Watch on YouTube

Meet Arjun Ramdeen, a Form 5 student and leg-spinner from Penal, south Trinidad. He scored 38% in Form 4 maths and says he is "not a maths person". In his first session we set his goal, look at the whole exam, and run a diagnostic test.

In this session

  • Arjun's story: cricket, the states of emergency, and the deal with his mother
  • His goal: Grade II or better, aiming for Grade I
  • CSEC Mathematics at a glance: Paper 01, Paper 02 and the SBA
  • Eight diagnostic items: pause and try them yourself before Arjun answers
  • After the call: my notes on what the evidence shows

Arjun: 2/11 on these eight; 15/30 on the whole test. Statistics: excellent!
